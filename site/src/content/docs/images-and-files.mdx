---
title: Images, sound and other files
description: Adding pictures, sound, video and fonts to a story
---

A story can show images, play sound and video, and use its own fonts. The files go in your story's folder, and they're included when you [publish](/publishing/) it.

## Adding files

- **In the editor:** the image button in the toolbar opens **Images and other files**, where you can add images, sounds and fonts. **Insert** puts an image into your story at the cursor, as an `<img>` tag.
- **With the command-line tool:** put the files next to your `.squiffy` file, or in a folder below it. See [Images and other files](/cli/#images-and-other-files) for how each output format includes them.

## Using files in your story

Use Markdown's image syntax:

```squiffy
![A sleeping cat](cat.png)
```

Or use HTML, for example for sound and video:

```squiffy
<img src="images/map.png" alt="Map of the island">
<audio src="sounds/rain.mp3" controls></audio>
```

Paths are relative to the story's main file, whichever file they're in. Images are scaled down to fit the page width (see [Customising styles](/styling/#page-layout)).

To use a font, add the font file and refer to it from your own [stylesheet](/styling/#where-to-put-your-styles) with `@font-face`.
