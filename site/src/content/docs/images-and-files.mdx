---
title: Images, sound and other files
description: Adding pictures, sound, video and fonts to a story
---

A story can show images, play sound and video, and use its own fonts. The files go in your story's folder, and they're included when you [publish](/publishing/) it.

## Adding files

- **In the editor:** the image button in the toolbar opens **Images and other files**, where you can add images, sounds and fonts. **Insert** puts an image into your story at the cursor, as an `<img>` tag.
- **With the command-line tool:** put the files in your story's folder, or a folder below it. See [Images and other files](/cli/#images-and-other-files) for how each output format includes them.

## Using files in your story

Use Markdown's image syntax:

```squiffy
![A sleeping cat](cat.png)
```

Or use HTML, for example for video:

```squiffy
<img src="images/map.png" alt="Map of the island">
<video src="intro.mp4" controls></video>
```

Paths are relative to the story's main file, whichever file they're in. Images are scaled down to fit the page width (see [Customising styles](/styling/#page-layout)).

For music and sound effects, see [Music and sound](#music-and-sound) below.

To use a font, add it with the editor's **Fonts** button, or add its files yourself and refer to them from your own stylesheet with `@font-face`. See [Fonts](/styling/#fonts).

## Music and sound

Play music with `@music`, at the start of a section or passage:

```squiffy
[[forest]]:
@music sounds/forest.mp3
Tall trees surround you. [[Go into the cave]](cave)

[[cave]]:
@music sounds/cave.mp3
@sound sounds/drip.mp3
Water drips from the roof. [[Leave]](outside)

[[outside]]:
@music stop
Daylight at last.
```

- **`@music file`** plays music, over and over, until another `@music` changes it. It keeps playing as the player moves from one section to the next, and if a section has the music that's already playing, it carries on rather than starting again. When the music changes, the old music fades out over a second while the new music fades in.
- **`@music stop`** fades the music out.
- **`@sound file`** plays a sound once, such as a door closing.
- **`@sound stop`** stops the sounds that are playing once. The music carries on.

To play music or a sound part of the way through the text, or only sometimes, use the helpers. Like other [helpers](/helpers-reference/#sound-helpers), they respect `{{#if}}`:

```squiffy
{{#if door_open}}{{sound "creak.mp3"}}{{/if}}
{{music "battle.mp3" fade=3}}
{{stopmusic fade=0}}
{{stopsounds}}
```

`fade=` is how many seconds the change takes, and `fade=0` changes the music straight away. `{{sound}}` can have a `volume=`, from 0 (silent) to 1 (the default): `{{sound "whisper.mp3" volume=0.5}}`.

The music playing is part of the story, like its attributes: the **Back** button puts back the music that was playing before, and a saved game plays its music when it's loaded. The `_music` attribute has its file name in it. A sound played with `@sound` or `{{sound}}` doesn't play again when the player goes back.

A story with music or sounds has a mute button above it, and the player's choice is remembered for the story. Browsers don't let a page play sound until the player has clicked something, so music that should be playing when the story starts begins at their first click.

The sound files go in your story's folder, like images, and are included when you publish it. They start loading when the story starts, so they're ready to play when they're needed. Use MP3 for the widest browser support.
