## Try it

This is a complete Squiffy story. Play it on the right, or change the text on the left and watch it update.

<Sample script="You wake up in a small boat, drifting on a calm sea. In the distance you can see an [island], and behind you, [[the open ocean]].

[island]:
A thin column of smoke rises from the trees. Someone is there. Perhaps you should [[row towards it]].

[[row towards it]]:
You row until your arms ache, and the boat scrapes onto a sandy beach.

[[the open ocean]]:
You drift for hours. The island grows smaller, and then it's gone."></Sample>

Links in `[[double brackets]]` move the story on to a new section. Links in `[single brackets]` show a passage, a little more text within the same section. That's all you need to get started.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Write in plain text" icon="pencil">
		Write your story as ordinary text, and add links where the player makes a choice. There's no programming needed.

		Format your text with Markdown, for **bold**, *italics*, lists and more, or use HTML when you need complete control. See [Basic concepts](/basic-concepts/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Choices that matter" icon="setting">
		Use [attributes](/attributes/) to remember what the player has done, and show different text depending on their choices, their score, or how many times they've [been somewhere](/attributes/#the-visits-helper). Some can even be [remembered between playthroughs](/attributes/#attributes-that-last-between-playthroughs).

		Keep track of gold, health and inventories with [calculations](/attributes/#calculating-values), and add variety with [random text, rotating links and timers](/dynamic-text/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Write wherever you like" icon="laptop">
		[The editor](/editor/) runs in your web browser, with nothing to install and no account to create, and works offline. The [desktop app](/desktop-app/), for Mac, Windows and Linux, keeps your stories in folders on your computer.

		Or write in [Visual Studio Code](/vscode/), with the Squiffy extension: your story plays beside your script, and you get the story map, the same checks as you type, and export. It works on [vscode.dev](https://vscode.dev) too.
	</Card>
	<Card title="An editor that knows your story" icon="magnifier">
		As you type, the editor underlines links to missing sections, dead ends, sections nobody can reach, and typing mistakes such as `{{glod}}` for `{{gold}}`, often with a quick fix. It suggests section names and helpers as you type, and renames a section everywhere at once.

		[Start the story at any section](/testing/#starting-at-a-later-section) to try the scene you're working on, or [set up a scene](/testing/#setting-up-a-scene) for testing, with the attributes it needs.
	</Card>
	<Card title="See your story as a map" icon="random">
		The [story map](/story-map/) draws your sections and links as you write, with problems marked in red and orange, and the way you went when you test the story.

		Add links by dragging between boxes, add sections and passages, rename and delete them, and search them by their text. Or make the map full screen and [write each section beside it](/story-map/#writing-on-the-map), as you would in Twine.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your story to life" icon="star">
		Add [music and sound](/images-and-files/#music-and-sound) and [videos](/images-and-files/#videos), make each scene [fade or slide in](/dynamic-text/#transitions), make text [type itself out](/dynamic-text/#animation), or make words [shudder, flicker or blur](/dynamic-text/#text-effects). [Change the look](/styling/#changing-the-look-for-a-scene) of a scene, and choose from over 2,000 [fonts](/styling/#fonts).

		Ask for the player's name, or anything else, with built-in [validation](/player-input/#input-validation).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Made for players" icon="mobile-android">
		Your finished story runs in the player's web browser, on phones, tablets and computers alike, with nothing to download or install.

		Players can go [back](/buttons-panels-settings/#the-back-button) a step, keep as many [saved games](/buttons-panels-settings/#saved-games) as they like, and read in [light or dark mode](/styling/#light-and-dark-mode). Show their stats or inventory in a [panel](/buttons-panels-settings/#panels) beside the story, and let them choose [settings](/buttons-panels-settings/#player-settings) such as content warnings.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Accessible from the start" icon="approve-check">
		Every story can be played with just the keyboard, and with a screen reader, which reads out the new text after each click. Links are underlined rather than told apart by colour alone, and animations calm down for players who've asked their system for less motion.

		You don't need to do anything to get this. [Writing an accessible story](/accessibility/) has a few habits for the rest, such as alt text for images.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Share it in seconds" icon="rocket">
		Click **Share** in the editor to [make a link](/publishing/#sharing-a-link) with your whole story in it, and send it to anyone. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to make: the link plays your story on squiffystories.com.

		Or [publish it to textadventures.co.uk](/publishing/#publishing-to-textadventurescouk) in one step, where players can find it. Squiffy creates plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can also put your story on [itch.io](/publishing/#publishing-on-itchio), your own website, or anywhere else that hosts web pages, even as a single HTML file.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your stories with you" icon="document">
		Open a [Twine](/converting-from-twine/) story, written in Harlowe or SugarCube, or a [Quest](/converting-from-quest/) gamebook, and the editor converts it to Squiffy. Wondering whether to switch from Twine? See [Why Squiffy?](/coming-from-twine/#why-squiffy)

		Stories from [Squiffy 6](/coming-from-squiffy-6/) still work, and Squiffy can convert most of a [Squiffy 5](/coming-from-squiffy-5/) story for you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Test, automate and extend" icon="puzzle">
		The [command-line tool](/cli/) checks your story for problems, plays it [at random](/testing/#playing-at-random) to find errors and dead ends, and replays [playthrough tests](/testing/#playthrough-tests) to check it still works after a change. An [AI agent](/writing-with-ai/) such as Claude Code can use it to check and play your story as it helps you write.

		When you need more, add [JavaScript](/javascript/) to any section or passage, create your own [helpers](/javascript/#creating-custom-helpers), or [embed your story](/embedding/) in your own web page or app.
	</Card>
	<Card title="It's free - and so are you" icon="heart">
		Squiffy is free and open source, licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/blob/main/LICENSE).

		The stories you make are yours, to share or to sell. You don't need to ask for permission - you already have it.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
