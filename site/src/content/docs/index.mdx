## Try it

This is a complete Squiffy story. Play it on the right, or change the text on the left and watch it update.

<Sample script="You wake up in a small boat, drifting on a calm sea. In the distance you can see an [island], and behind you, [[the open ocean]].

[island]:
A thin column of smoke rises from the trees. Someone is there. Perhaps you should [[row towards it]].

[[row towards it]]:
You row until your arms ache, and the boat scrapes onto a sandy beach.

[[the open ocean]]:
You drift for hours. The island grows smaller, and then it's gone."></Sample>

Links in `[[double brackets]]` move the story on to a new section. Links in `[single brackets]` show a passage, a little more text within the same section. That's all you need to get started.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Write in plain text" icon="pencil">
		Write your story as ordinary text, and add links where the player makes a choice. There's no programming needed.

		Format your text with Markdown, for **bold**, *italics*, lists and more, or use HTML when you need complete control. See [Basic concepts](/basic-concepts/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Choices that matter" icon="setting">
		Use [attributes](/attributes/) to remember what the player has done, then show different text depending on their choices, their score, or which parts of the story they've already seen.

		Add variety with [random text, rotating links and text that updates itself](/dynamic-text/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your story to life" icon="star">
		Make text appear with a typewriter effect, fade in, or slide in from below, or let the reader click to continue at their own pace. See [Animation](/dynamic-text/#animation).

		Ask for the player's name, or any other input, with built-in [validation](/basic-concepts/#input-validation).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Write anywhere" icon="laptop">
		[The editor](/editor/) runs in your web browser, with nothing to install and no account to create. Install it to your device and it works offline, too.

		A downloadable desktop app is coming soon, in Squiffy 7.

		Prefer your own text editor? Use the [command-line tool](/cli/) instead.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Plays on any device" icon="mobile-android">
		Your finished story runs in the player's web browser, on phones, tablets and computers alike. There's nothing for them to download or install.

		Players' progress is saved automatically, so they can close the page and come back later to carry on where they left off.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Share with the world" icon="rocket">
		Squiffy creates plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can [publish your story](/getting-started/#publishing-your-game) to [textadventures.co.uk](https://textadventures.co.uk/), [itch.io](https://itch.io/), your own website, or anywhere else that hosts web pages.

		You can even export it as a single HTML file, to share however you like.

		See what other people are making in the [catalogue of Squiffy games](https://textadventures.co.uk/games/platform/squiffy).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Extend with JavaScript" icon="seti:javascript">
		When you need more, add [JavaScript](/basic-concepts/#javascript) to any section or passage, create your own [helpers](/helpers-reference/#creating-custom-helpers) and animations, or [embed your story](/embedding/) in your own web page or app.
	</Card>
	<Card title="It's free - and so are you" icon="heart">
		Squiffy is free and open source, licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/blob/main/LICENSE).

		The stories you make are yours, to share or to sell. You don't need to ask for permission - you already have it.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
