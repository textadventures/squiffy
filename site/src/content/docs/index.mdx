## Try it

This is a complete Squiffy story. Play it on the right, or change the text on the left and watch it update.

<Sample script="You wake up in a small boat, drifting on a calm sea. In the distance you can see an [island], and behind you, [[the open ocean]].

[island]:
A thin column of smoke rises from the trees. Someone is there. Perhaps you should [[row towards it]].

[[row towards it]]:
You row until your arms ache, and the boat scrapes onto a sandy beach.

[[the open ocean]]:
You drift for hours. The island grows smaller, and then it's gone."></Sample>

Links in `[[double brackets]]` move the story on to a new section. Links in `[single brackets]` show a passage, a little more text within the same section. That's all you need to get started.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Write in plain text" icon="pencil">
		Write your story as ordinary text, and add links where the player makes a choice. There's no programming needed.

		Format your text with Markdown, for **bold**, *italics*, lists and more, or use HTML when you need complete control. See [Basic concepts](/basic-concepts/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Choices that matter" icon="setting">
		Use [attributes](/attributes/) to remember what the player has done, and show different text depending on their choices, their score, or how many times they've [been somewhere](/attributes/#the-visits-helper).

		Keep track of gold, health and inventories with [calculations](/attributes/#calculating-values), and add variety with [random text, rotating links and timers](/dynamic-text/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="An editor that knows your story" icon="laptop">
		[The editor](/editor/) draws your story as a [map](/story-map/) as you write, and underlines links to missing sections, dead ends and sections nobody can reach, often with a quick fix. Rename a section everywhere at once, or test from any section.

		It runs in your web browser, with nothing to install and no account to create, and works offline. Or download the [desktop app](/desktop-app/), for Mac, Windows and Linux.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your story to life" icon="star">
		Add [music and sound](/images-and-files/#music-and-sound), make each scene [fade or slide in](/dynamic-text/#transitions), make text [type itself out](/dynamic-text/#animation), or make words [shudder, flicker or blur](/dynamic-text/#text-effects). [Change the look](/styling/#changing-the-look-for-a-scene) of a scene, and choose from over 2,000 [fonts](/styling/#fonts).

		Ask for the player's name, or anything else, with built-in [validation](/player-input/#input-validation).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Made for players" icon="mobile-android">
		Your finished story runs in the player's web browser, on phones, tablets and computers alike, with nothing to download or install.

		Players can go [back](/buttons-panels-settings/#the-back-button) a step, keep as many [saved games](/buttons-panels-settings/#saved-games) as they like, and read in [light or dark mode](/styling/#light-and-dark-mode). Show their stats or inventory in a [panel](/buttons-panels-settings/#panels) beside the story, and let them choose [settings](/buttons-panels-settings/#player-settings) such as content warnings.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Share with the world" icon="rocket">
		Squiffy creates plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can [publish your story](/publishing/) to [textadventures.co.uk](https://textadventures.co.uk/), [itch.io](https://itch.io/), your own website, or anywhere else that hosts web pages. You can even export it as a single HTML file.

		See what other people are making in the [catalogue of Squiffy games](https://textadventures.co.uk/games/platform/squiffy).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your stories with you" icon="document">
		Open a [Twine](/converting-from-twine/) story, written in Harlowe or SugarCube, or a [Quest](/converting-from-quest/) gamebook, and the editor converts it to Squiffy. Wondering whether to switch from Twine? See [Why Squiffy?](/coming-from-twine/#why-squiffy)

		Stories from [Squiffy 6](/coming-from-squiffy-6/) still work, and Squiffy can convert most of a [Squiffy 5](/coming-from-squiffy-5/) story for you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Test it, or let AI help" icon="approve-check">
		The [command-line tool](/cli/) checks your story for problems, plays it [at random](/cli/#playing-at-random) to find errors and dead ends, and replays [playthrough tests](/cli/#testing-a-story) to check it still works after a change.

		Write with an [AI agent](/writing-with-ai/) such as Claude Code, and it can check and play your story as it goes.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Extend with JavaScript" icon="seti:javascript">
		When you need more, add [JavaScript](/javascript/) to any section or passage, create your own [helpers](/javascript/#creating-custom-helpers) and animations, or [embed your story](/embedding/) in your own web page or app.
	</Card>
	<Card title="It's free - and so are you" icon="heart">
		Squiffy is free and open source, licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/blob/main/LICENSE).

		The stories you make are yours, to share or to sell. You don't need to ask for permission - you already have it.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
