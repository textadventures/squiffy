Squiffy is a tool for creating interactive fiction - that is,
multiple choice games that focus on text and story. Players
navigate through the game or story by clicking links.

Squiffy is free and open source. It creates HTML and JavaScript,
so you can [publish your story](/publishing/) on your own website, or on a site like
<a href="https://textadventures.co.uk">textadventures.co.uk</a>
or <a href="https://itch.io/">itch.io</a>.

## Where to write your story

There are four ways to write a Squiffy story. They all use the same language, so you can move a story from one to another.

- **The [editor](/editor/)** at [app.squiffystory.com](https://app.squiffystory.com/) runs in your web browser, with nothing to install and no account to create. A new story is kept in your browser's storage, which the browser can clear, so download a copy now and then with **File > Download project (.zip)**. In Chrome or Edge, you can open a folder on your computer instead, and the editor saves to the files there.
- **The [desktop app](/desktop-app/)**, for Mac, Windows and Linux, is the same editor, and keeps your stories in folders on your computer.
- **[VS Code](/vscode/)**, with Squiffy's extension, shows problems as you type and plays your story beside your script, if you'd rather write in a code editor.
- **The [command-line tool](/cli/)** turns a `.squiffy` file you've written in your own text editor into a game.

## Where to go next

- [Tutorial: your first story](/tutorial/) walks you through writing a short story, from the first link to publishing it, and [Tutorial: make it your own](/tutorial-part-2/) gives it its own look, sound and a panel.
- [Basic concepts](/basic-concepts/) covers sections, passages and links, and the rest of **Writing stories** goes on from there.
- [Syntax cheat sheet](/syntax/) has everything you can write in a story, on one page.
- [Cookbook](/cookbook/) has ready-made patterns, such as an inventory, a shop and random events.
- [Examples](/featured-examples/) has complete stories to play, and to learn from.
- [Publishing your story](/publishing/) explains how to share it with the world.
- [Writing with an AI agent](/writing-with-ai/) explains how Claude Code, or another agent, can help you write, check and play your story.
- Coming from somewhere else? See [Coming from Twine](/coming-from-twine/), [Converting a Quest gamebook](/converting-from-quest/), [Coming from Squiffy 6](/coming-from-squiffy-6/) or [Coming from Squiffy 5](/coming-from-squiffy-5/).

## Getting help

You can ask questions and discuss Squiffy
on [Discord](https://textadventures.co.uk/community/discord),
at [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/discussions),
or at [intfiction.org](https://intfiction.org/tag/squiffy) (using the `squiffy` tag).

If you find a bug, please log an [issue](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues). Squiffy's source code is in its [GitHub repository](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy).
