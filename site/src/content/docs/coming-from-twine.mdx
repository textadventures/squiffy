If you've written stories with [Twine](https://twinery.org/), much of Squiffy will feel familiar: a story is made of named chunks of text, joined by links, with variables to remember what the player has done. This page explains what's different, and how to write in Squiffy the things you'd write in Harlowe or SugarCube.

If you already have a Twine story, Squiffy can convert it for you: see [Converting a Twine story](/converting-from-twine/).

## The same story, three ways

Here's a short story in Harlowe, SugarCube and Squiffy. The player finds a key in a drawer, and needs it to open a door.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Harlowe">
```text
:: Hall
You're in a dusty hall. There's a (link-reveal: "drawer")[ with a key in it(set: $hasKey to true)], and a [[door->Door]].

:: Door
(if: $hasKey)[You unlock the door and step outside. [[Go out->Outside]]](else:)[The door is locked. [[Go back->Hall]]]

:: Outside
Fresh air, at last.
```
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="SugarCube">
```text
:: Hall
You're in a dusty hall. There's a <<linkappend "drawer">> with a key in it<<set $hasKey to true>><</linkappend>>, and a [[door|Door]].

:: Door
<<if $hasKey>>You unlock the door and step outside. [[Go out|Outside]]
<<else>>The door is locked. [[Go back|Hall]]<</if>>

:: Outside
Fresh air, at last.
```
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

And in Squiffy, which you can play and edit here:

<Sample script="[[Hall]]:
You're in a dusty hall. There's a [drawer], and a [[door]].

[drawer]:
@set hasKey
You find a key in the drawer.

[[door]]:
{{#if hasKey}}
You unlock the door and step outside. [[Go out]](Outside)
{{else}}
The door is locked. [[Go back]](Hall)
{{/if}}

[[Outside]]:
@end
Fresh air, at last."></Sample>

A few things to notice:

- Twine's passages are Squiffy's **sections**. A section starts with its name in double square brackets and a colon, `[[Hall]]:`, rather than a `::` line or a box on the story map.
- The drawer is a Squiffy **passage**: a smaller piece of text that appears in the same section when the player clicks its link. Twine has nothing quite like it. The nearest thing is a link that reveals text, like `(link-reveal:)` or `<<linkappend>>`.
- Variables don't start with `$`. Squiffy calls them **attributes**.
- When the player goes back to the hall, it doesn't replace the door. It's added below it, and the drawer can be opened again. More on that in [The story scrolls](#the-story-scrolls) below.

## What's different

### "Passage" means something else

This is the one to get used to first. Squiffy has two kinds of text block:

- A **section**, `[[name]]:`, is what Twine calls a passage. Links to it use double square brackets, `[[name]]`, and following one moves the story on.
- A **passage**, `[name]:`, is a smaller piece of text that belongs to a section. Links to it use single square brackets, `[name]`. Its text appears in the same section, and the section's other links still work, so the player can explore passages in any order.

Passages are good for looking around a room, asking a character questions, or anything else that doesn't move the story on. See [Basic concepts](/basic-concepts/).

### The story scrolls

A Twine story usually shows one passage at a time. A Squiffy story is more like a transcript: each new section is added below the last, so the player can scroll back and read the story so far.

When the player moves to a new section, the links in the old ones stop working. To choose differently, the player can go back: see [Going back and saving](#going-back-and-saving).

If you'd rather show one section at a time, clear the screen before each one, with a [master section](/basic-concepts/#master-sections-and-passages), which runs before every section:

```squiffy
[[]]:
@clear
```

### Going back and saving

As in Harlowe and SugarCube, players can undo their choices: a published Squiffy story has a **Back** button, which goes back a section or passage at a time, next to **Restart**. If you'd rather the player's choices were final, you can turn it off: see [The Back button](/multiple-files/#the-back-button).

The player's progress is saved automatically in their browser, so they can close the page and carry on where they left off when they come back. They can also keep as many saved games as they like, with the **Save** button: see [Saved games](/multiple-files/#saved-games).

### Setting attributes

Attributes don't start with `$`, and you set them with `@set`, on a line of its own at the start of a section or passage:

```squiffy
@set gold = 10
@set name = "Alice"
@set hasKey
```

Text goes in quotes, but numbers don't, and `@set hasKey` on its own sets it to `true`. See [Attributes](/attributes/).

<Aside type="caution">
An `@set` line always runs when its section or passage is shown, even if it's inside an `{{#if}}`. To change an attribute only when a condition is true, use the `{{set}}`, `{{inc}}` and `{{dec}}` helpers inside the `{{#if}}` instead, as you would `(set:)` or `<<set>>` in Twine. See [Conditional attribute changes](/attributes/#conditional-attribute-changes).
</Aside>

### Conditions and text

Squiffy's dynamic text uses [Handlebars](https://handlebarsjs.com/): things in `{{double curly brackets}}`, with blocks like `{{#if}}...{{/if}}` in place of Harlowe's hooks or SugarCube's `<<if>>...<</if>>`. Comparisons use `=` and `!=`, rather than `is` or `eq`, and `and`, `or` and `not` work as you'd expect. See [Dynamic text](/dynamic-text/).

### Formatting is Markdown

Text is formatted with [Markdown](https://daringfireball.net/projects/markdown/syntax) and HTML, rather than Twine's markup: `**bold**` and `_italic_`, rather than `''bold''` and `//italic//`.

In Markdown, a single line break is just a space. Leave a blank line between paragraphs, as you would in an email.

### Indented lines are JavaScript

Lines indented with four spaces or a tab, at the start of a section or passage, are [JavaScript](/javascript/). So don't indent the text of your story.

### One file, not boxes

A Squiffy story is a text file, more like Twee than Twine's boxes. The [editor](/editor/) helps you find your way around it: the outline lists every section and passage, and the [story map](/story-map/) shows them as boxes with links between them, much like Twine's. You can add sections and links from the map too.

### One story format

There's one Squiffy language, so there's no story format to choose.

## Phrasebook

How to write in Squiffy what you'd write in Harlowe or SugarCube.

### Links and passages

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start a passage | `:: Cave` | `:: Cave` | `[[Cave]]:` |
| Link to a passage | `[[Go in->Cave]]` | `[[Go in\|Cave]]` | `[[Go in]](Cave)` |
| Reveal some text in place | `(link-reveal: "Open")[...]` | `<<linkappend "Open">>...<</linkappend>>` | A passage: `[Open]`, and `[Open]:` |
| Include another passage | `(display: "Map")` | `<<include "Map">>` | `{{embed "Map"}}` |
| Go to a passage without a click | `(go-to: "End")` | `<<goto "End">>` | `{{goto "End"}}` |
| ...after a delay | `(after: 2s)[(go-to: "End")]` | `<<timed 2s>><<goto "End">><</timed>>` | `{{goto "End" after=2000}}` |
| Set the start passage | In Twine's story map | In Twine's story map | `@start Cave`, or put it first |
| Set the story's title | The story's name in Twine | The story's name in Twine | `@title My Story` |

### Variables

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set a variable | `(set: $gold to 10)` | `<<set $gold to 10>>` | `@set gold = 10` |
| ...only if a condition is true | `(set:)` in a hook | `<<set>>` inside `<<if>>` | `{{set "gold" 10}}` inside `{{#if}}` |
| Add to a number | `(set: $gold to it + 5)` | `<<set $gold += 5>>` | `@inc gold 5`, or `{{inc "gold" 5}}` |
| Show a variable | `$name` | `$name` | `{{name}}` |
| Show a calculation | `(print: $gold * 2)` | `<<print $gold * 2>>` | `{{= gold * 2}}` |
| Set a variable from a link | `(link: "Buy")[(set: $gold to it - 5)(go-to: "Shop")]` | `[[Buy\|Shop][$gold -= 5]]` | `[[Buy]](Shop, gold -= 5)` |

### Conditions

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| If and else | `(if: $gold > 5)[...](else:)[...]` | `<<if $gold gt 5>>...<<else>>...<</if>>` | `{{#if gold > 5}}...{{else}}...{{/if}}` |
| Else if | `(else-if: $gold > 2)[...]` | `<<elseif $gold gt 2>>` | `{{else if gold > 2}}` |
| Compare | `$a is 1`, `$a is not 1` | `$a is 1`, `$a isnot 1` | `a = 1`, `a != 1` |
| Check a passage has been seen | `(history:) contains "Cave"` | `visited("Cave")` | `(seen "Cave")` |
| Count visits | `(count: (history:), "Cave")` | `visited("Cave")` | `(visits "Cave")` |
| Count turns | | `turns()` | `turns` |
| The passage before this one | `(history:)'s last` | `previous()` | `previous` |

### Changing text and links

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mark some text to change later | `\|door>[closed]` | `<span id="door">closed</span>` | `{{#label "door"}}closed{{/label}}` |
| Change it | `(replace: ?door)[open]` | `<<replace "#door">>open<</replace>>` | `{{#replace "door"}}open{{/replace}}` |
| Random text | `(either: "a", "b")` | `<<print either("a", "b")>>` | `{{random (array "a" "b")}}` |
| A link that cycles through options | `(cycling-link: bind $size, "S", "L")` | `<<cycle "$size">><<option "S">><<option "L">><</cycle>>` | `{{rotate (array "S" "L") set="size"}}` |
| A text box | `(input-box: bind $name)` | `<<textbox "$name" "">>` | `<input data-attribute="name">` |

See [Dynamic text](/dynamic-text/) and [Player input](/player-input/).

### Arrays

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make an array | `(set: $bag to (a: "key"))` | `<<set $bag to ["key"]>>` | `{{set "bag" (array "key")}}` |
| Add to it | `(set: $bag to it + (a: "map"))` | `<<run $bag.push("map")>>` | `{{append "bag" "map"}}` |
| Check what's in it | `$bag contains "key"` | `$bag.includes("key")` | `bag contains "key"` |
| Count what's in it | `$bag's length` | `$bag.length` | `bag.length` |
| Get an item | `$bag's 1st`, `$bag's last`, `$bag's ($i)` | `$bag[0]`, `$bag.last()`, `$bag[$i]` | `bag[0]`, `(last bag)`, `bag[i]` (positions start at 0) |
| Write it as a list | `(joined: ", ", ...$bag)` | `$bag.join(", ")` | `(join bag ", " " and ")` |
| Sort or shuffle it | `(sorted: ...$bag)`, `(shuffled: ...$bag)` | `$bag.sort()`, `$bag.shuffle()` | `(sort bag)`, `(shuffle bag)` |
| Take part of it | `$bag's (range: 2, 3)` | `$bag.slice(1, 3)` | `(slice bag 1 3)` |
| Find or count an item | `(count: $bag, "coin")` | `$bag.indexOf("key")`, `$bag.count("coin")` | `(indexof bag "key")`, `(count bag "coin")` |
| Do something for each item | `(for: each _item, ...$bag)[_item]` | `<<for _item range $bag>>_item<</for>>` | `{{#each bag}}{{this}}{{/each}}` |
| Count from one number to another | `(for: each _i, ...(range: 1, $n))[_i]` | `<<for _i to 1; _i lte $n; _i++>>_i<</for>>` | `{{#each (range 1 n)}}{{this}}{{/each}}` |
| A link for each item | `(for: each _item, ...$bag)[(link: _item)[A _item.]]` | `<<for _item range $bag>><<link _item>>A _item.<</link>><</for>>` | `{{#each bag}}[{{this}}](look, item = "{{this}}"){{/each}}`, with `A {{item}}.` in the passage `[look]` |

See [Array management helpers](/helpers-reference/#array-management-helpers) and [Going through an array](/attributes/#going-through-an-array).

### Text and numbers

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change text to capitals or small letters | `(uppercase: $name)`, `(lowercase: $name)` | `$name.toUpperCase()`, `$name.toLowerCase()` | `(upper name)`, `(lower name)` |
| Give text a capital first letter | `(upperfirst: $name)` | `$name.toUpperFirst()` | `(capitalise name)` |
| Check whether text has other text in it | `$name contains "Mac"` | `$name.includes("Mac")` | `name contains "Mac"` |
| Count the letters | `$name's length` | `$name.length` | `name.length` |
| Round a number | `(round: $x)`, `(floor: $x)`, `(ceil: $x)` | `Math.round($x)`, `Math.floor($x)`, `Math.ceil($x)` | `(round x)`, `(floor x)`, `(ceil x)` |
| The smallest or largest | `(min: $a, $b)`, `(max: $a, $b)` | `Math.min($a, $b)`, `Math.max($a, $b)` | `(min a b)`, `(max a b)` |
| Choose between two values | `(cond: $hp > 0, "alive", "dead")` | `$hp > 0 ? "alive" : "dead"` | `hp > 0 ? "alive" : "dead"` |

See [Calculations](/helpers-reference/#calculations), [Maths helpers](/helpers-reference/#maths-helpers) and [Text helpers](/helpers-reference/#text-helpers).

### Objects

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make an object | `(set: $hero to (dm: "hp", 10, "name", "Ann"))` | `<<set $hero to { hp: 10, name: "Ann" }>>` | `@set hero = { hp: 10, name: "Ann" }` |
| Show a value in it | `$hero's hp` | `$hero.hp` | `{{hero.hp}}` |
| Change a value in it | `(set: $hero's hp to it + 5)` | `<<set $hero.hp += 5>>` | `@set hero.hp += 5` |
| Use a name in a variable | `$hero's ($stat)` | `$hero[$stat]` | `hero[stat]` |
| An array of objects | `$party's 1st's name` | `$party[0].name` | `{{= party[0].name}}` |

See [Objects](/attributes/#objects).

### Sound

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Play music that carries on | `(track: "theme", "loop", true)` and `(track: "theme", "play")` | `<<audio "theme" loop play>>` | `@music theme.mp3` |
| Stop the music | `(track: "theme", "stop")` | `<<audio "theme" stop>>` | `@music stop` |
| Play a sound once | `(track: "door", "play")` | `<<audio "door" play>>` | `@sound door.mp3`, or `{{sound "door.mp3"}}` |
| Stop the sounds | `(track: "door", "stop")` | `<<audio "door" stop>>` | `@sound stop`, or `{{stopsounds}}`, which stops every sound playing once |

Squiffy fades from one track to the next by itself, and players get a mute button above the story. See [Music and sound](/images-and-files/#music-and-sound).

### Special passages

| To... | Harlowe | SugarCube | Squiffy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Show something before every passage | A passage tagged `header` | `PassageHeader` | A [master section](/basic-concepts/#master-sections-and-passages), `[[]]:` |
| Run something when the story starts | A passage tagged `startup` | `StoryInit` | `@set` lines in the start section, or JavaScript in a [`@ui` block](/javascript/#running-code-when-the-story-starts) |
| Add JavaScript | Story JavaScript | Story JavaScript | A [`@ui` block](/javascript/#running-code-when-the-story-starts), or indented lines in a section |
| Add CSS | Story Stylesheet | Story Stylesheet | A `.css` file in the story's folder: see [Customising styles](/styling/) |
| Show something in the sidebar | `(replace: ?sidebar)` | `StoryCaption`, `StoryMenu` | A [panel](/multiple-files/#panels), beside the story or opened from a button in the header |
| Change the look for a scene | Tag the passage, and style `tw-story[tags~="dark"]` | Tag the passage, and style `body[data-tags~="dark"]` | `@class dark`, and style `body:has(.dark)`: see [Changing the look for a scene](/styling/#changing-the-look-for-a-scene) |
| Add a class to the page | `(enchant: ?page, ...)` | `<<addclass "body" "dark">>`, `<<removeclass>>`, `<<toggleclass>>` | `{{addclass "dark"}}`, `{{removeclass "dark"}}`, `{{toggleclass "dark"}}` |
| Check a passage's tags | `(passage:)'s tags contains "dark"` | `tags().includes("dark")` | `{{#if (hasclass "dark")}}` |
| Write a comment | `<!-- ... -->` | `/* ... */` | `{{!-- ... --}}` |

## What Twine has that Squiffy doesn't

Some things in Harlowe and SugarCube aren't in Squiffy yet. Each one is planned, and its GitHub issue says how it might work, so you can follow it or add your thoughts:

- **Widgets**, written in your story rather than JavaScript, with their own parameters ([#450](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues/450)). [`{{embed}}`](/helpers-reference/#content-helpers) includes another section, without parameters.
- **Timers** that repeat, or wait for a time worked out as the story runs, like `<<repeat>>`, `(live:)` and `<<timed>>` ([#451](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues/451)). Squiffy can [move on after a fixed delay](/dynamic-text/#moving-on-after-a-delay).
- **Links in the text that restart the story or open the saved games**, and player settings, like SugarCube's `UI` and `Setting` ([#452](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues/452)). They're on the buttons above the story.
- **More text effects**, like Harlowe's `"shudder"` and `"blur"`, and a short way to style some text, like SugarCube's `@@.class;...@@` ([#453](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues/453)). Use HTML and [CSS](/styling/), and [`{{#animate}}`](/dynamic-text/#animation).
- **A text box that moves on when Enter is pressed** ([#454](https://github.com/textadventures/squiffy/issues/454)).

### Left out on purpose

- **Temporary variables**, like SugarCube's `_name`. In Twine, they're mostly for loop counters and values worked out along the way. In Squiffy, `{{this}}` and `{{@index}}` give you a loop's item and position, and since every attribute is saved and undone by the Back button, a value can't be left behind out of step with the story.
- **Loops that aren't over an array**, like `<<for _i lt 10>>`, and `<<break>>` and `<<continue>>`. A section's text is worked out each time it's shown, and a loop that never ended would freeze the page. Go through an array with [`{{#each}}`](/attributes/#going-through-an-array), and use `{{#if}}` inside it to skip items.
- **A link back to where the player came from**, like `<<return>>`. In Twine, it's mostly for screens the player visits and leaves, like an inventory, map or help page. Squiffy's story scrolls, so going back to a section shows it again below the screen the player left, rather than putting them back where they were. Use a [panel](/multiple-files/#panels) instead: it's beside the story or opened from a button in the header, so the player never leaves the story to see it. To link back from one section to the one before, use `{{section previous_section text="Return"}}`: see [Where the player came from](/attributes/#where-the-player-came-from).
- **Harlowe's and SugarCube's JavaScript**, such as `State`, `setup` and `(macro:)`. It's written for those formats' engines. Squiffy has [its own JavaScript functions](/javascript/), which can do almost anything, including adding your own helpers.
