---
title: What's new in Squiffy 7
description: The new editor, the desktop app, calculations, and the other changes in Squiffy 7
---

Squiffy 7 brings a new editor. The language adds the [`@end`](/basic-concepts/#endings) directive for marking endings, [calculations](/helpers-reference/#calculations), and the same rules for `@set` as for `{{#if}}`. Squiffy 6 stories still work, and the editor converts them for you: see [Coming from Squiffy 6](/coming-from-squiffy-6/). See [Using the editor](/editor/) for a tour.

## The new editor

- **Stories in several files.** A story can be split across `.squiffy` files, each in its own tab. New stories have a [`squiffy.json` project file](/multiple-files/), so every `.squiffy` file in the folder is part of the story, with no `@import`s needed. The command-line tool and the Vite plugin read it too.
- **[Images and other files](/images-and-files/).** Add images, sounds and fonts to your story, and they're included when you publish it.
- **An editor that understands your story.** Problems such as links to missing sections, sections the player can't reach, and dead ends are underlined as you type, often with a quick fix. Links suggest your section and passage names as you type them. You can go from a link to its section, find every link to a section, and rename a section or passage everywhere at once.
- **A [story map](/story-map/)** shows your sections, passages and links as a map you can move around, drawn from your script as you type. Problems show on it too, as does the way you went through the story when you test it. You can add links and sections, and rename them, from the map.
- **An outline** of your sections and passages, with a search box, replaces the section and passage dropdowns.
- **Run from any section**, so you can test the part you're working on without playing through from the start. As you click through the story, the script follows.
- **Stories in your browser**, without choosing a file, in every browser. Download one as a `.zip` to keep a copy.
- **Folders.** In Chrome and Edge, the editor can open a story's folder on your computer, and remembers the folders you've opened.
- **A dark theme**, and a text size setting. The preview follows it.
- **A [desktop app](/desktop-app/)** for Mac, Windows and Linux, with the same editor, which keeps your stories in folders on your computer.
- **Import from Twine.** Open a [Twine](https://twinery.org/) story, written in Harlowe or SugarCube, and the editor converts it to Squiffy. See [Converting a Twine story](/converting-from-twine/).

## The language

- **Calculations.** Work out values with `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and `%` in conditions, in `{{set}}`, `{{inc}}` and `{{dec}}`, and in the new `{{= ...}}`, which displays the result: `{{set "gold" gold + price * 2}}`, `{{#if gold >= price * 2}}`, or `That costs {{= price * 2}} gold.` There are matching [maths helpers](/helpers-reference/#maths-helpers) too: `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div` and `mod`. See [Calculating values](/attributes/#calculating-values).
- **`@set` and link assignments work like `{{#if}}`.** Text goes in quotes and attributes don't need an `@`, so a value can be a calculation or a comparison: `@set weapon = "sword"`, `@set gold = gold + price * 2`, `@set rich = gold > 100`, or `[[Buy]](shop, gold -= price)`. See [Setting attributes](/attributes/#setting-attributes).
- **Go to another section from your text.** [`{{goto "section"}}`](/basic-concepts/#go-to-another-section) moves the story on without a link, for example `{{#if health <= 0}}{{goto "death"}}{{/if}}`, and `{{goto "darkness" after=3000}}` does it after a delay.
- **Visit and turn counts.** [`{{visits "cave"}}`](/attributes/#the-visits-helper) says how many times the player has been to a section or passage, for example `{{#if (visits "cave") > 2}}`, and the [`turns` attribute](/attributes/#counting-turns) counts the links clicked in the whole story.
- **Where the player came from.** The [`previous_section` and `previous` attributes](/attributes/#where-the-player-came-from) say which section, and which section or passage, the player saw before this one, so a section can begin differently depending on how they got there: `{{#if previous_section = "river"}}`.
- **Endings.** Mark the sections where your story ends with [`@end`](/basic-concepts/#endings), and the editor won't report them as dead ends.

## Published stories

- **A Back button.** Players can undo their choices, a section or passage at a time. To leave it out, see [The Back button](/multiple-files/#the-back-button).
- **Dark mode.** Published stories follow the reader's light or dark system setting, and have a button to switch between them. To keep your story light, see [Light and dark mode](/styling/#light-and-dark-mode).
- **A fresher look.** The default style has more space between lines, softer colours, a modern serif font, and text sizes that follow the reader's browser setting. Its colours are [CSS variables](/styling/#colours), so they're easier to change.


## Coming from an earlier version

- **Squiffy 6:** your stories still work. See [Coming from Squiffy 6](/coming-from-squiffy-6/) for what's changed, and how the editor and the command-line tool convert a story to Squiffy 7's rules.
- **Squiffy 5:** Squiffy 6 was a complete rewrite, so there's more that's new. The text processor, for things like `{if ...}`, changed to [Handlebars](https://handlebarsjs.com/), and Squiffy can convert most of a story for you. The editor no longer needs a textadventures.co.uk account. Squiffy 6 also added [animation](/dynamic-text/#animation), [live text](/dynamic-text/#live), [player input](/player-input/) and single-file publishing. See [Coming from Squiffy 5](/coming-from-squiffy-5/).
